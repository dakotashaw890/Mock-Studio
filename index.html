What makes a good blank

  • Facing the camera straight on, with nothing in front of the chest: no crossed arms, long hair, or straps.
  • No logos, text, tags, or graphics on the garment. If any show up, regenerate.
  • Soft, even light. Hard shadows across the chest will show through the print.
  • Natural folds are good. Deep creases right across the print area look odd.
  • Generate one photo per shirt color you sell, so each listing matches the real product.
  • Check hands, fingers and the neckline before using an image.
  • Check that your image generator allows commercial use of its output.
